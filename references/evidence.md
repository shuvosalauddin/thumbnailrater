# Evidence and Confidence

1. **Measured image properties:** Dimensions, aspect ratio, grayscale tonal range, and tonal range after a 120x68 resize. These are descriptive image statistics, not quality guarantees.
2. **Human visual judgment:** Subject clarity, title-thumbnail fit, and cognitive simplicity must be assessed by inspecting the actual image in context. State when missing title or audience context limits a rating.
3. **Observed audience data:** CTR and impressions can describe performance in context. Calibration requires joining these with consistent human ratings; association is not causation and does not establish an expected CTR for a new thumbnail.

Keep measurements and judgments distinct in every report. Never claim saliency mapping, OCR, text-density detection, or a validated CTR prediction from these scripts.
