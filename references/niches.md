# Niche Considerations

Use these as prompts for visual review, not fixed rules or evidence that a style performs better.

## Science, Explainer & Documentary
- Consider whether a visual metaphor or anomaly is clear without explanation.
- Check that visual detail and color treatment support the idea rather than obscure it.

## Tech & Reviews
- Consider whether the product or relevant interface is identifiable at feed size.
- Check that dramatic treatments do not hide important product detail.

## History & Narrative Essays
- Consider whether imagery conveys the subject and period without relying on unsupported implications.
- Review dark scenes at feed size; atmosphere should not make the focal subject disappear.
