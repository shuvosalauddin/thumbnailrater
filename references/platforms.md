# Platform Checks

Layouts and thumbnail display sizes vary by device, surface, and platform changes. The preprocessing script's 120x68 image is a consistent small-preview stress test, not a guaranteed YouTube viewport.

- Inspect at a small size and check whether the main subject and any essential text remain recognizable.
- Keep essential details clear of the lower-right duration badge area.
- Check that the image remains distinct against both dark and light surrounding UI.
